Itinerary

Land in the summer capital, settle into your stays along the serene valley, meet your expedition leaders, and enjoy an evening orientation under the mountain twilight.

Leave the green meadows of Sonamarg behind and climb the rugged switchbacks of Zoji La (3,528m). Descend through Drass, the gateway to Ladakh, and unwind in historic Kargil.

Traverse Namika La and Fotu La, witness the lunar landscapes of Lamayuru, pay respects at Pathar Sahib Gurudwara, and watch the sacred confluence of the Indus and Zanskar rivers before reaching Leh.

Ascend the iconic Khardung La (5,359m) into the stark expanse of Nubra Valley. Pay homage at Diskit Monastery and watch the dusk settle over the cold desert dunes of Hunder.

Follow the winding turquoise path of the Shyok River through remote hamlets, breaking out onto the surreal, multi-hued shoreline of Pangong Tso for an lakeside wilderness stay.

Journey past the Rezang La War Memorial and cross Loma Bridge into the serene high-altitude plateau of Hanle, home to dark sky reserves and ancient stillness.

Ride to the roof of the planet over Photi La to reach Umling La (5,640m). Gaze over the desolate Tibetan borderlands and explore Demchok before returning to Hanle.

Traverse the wild, high plains to the quiet shores of Kyagar Tso and Tso Moriri. Soak in the solitude of Changthang before tracing the Indus back to Leh.

Ride out across Tanglang La (5,328m) and navigate the endless horizon of the More Plains. Descend through the 21 hairpin bends of Gata Loops to camp under the stars in Sarchu.

Conquer Baralacha La (4,850m) past the emerald waters of Suraj Tal. Trace through Jispa, Keylong, and the engineering marvel of the Atal Tunnel into lush Manali.

Spend a relaxed morning exploring Old Manali’s pine-shaded cafes or sacred springs before boarding your evening Volvo transfer towards Delhi.
