About This Trip

Stories aren't born inside the four walls of the city—they come alive amidst ancient deodar forests, over shared sips of steaming pahadi chai, and beside roaring mountain rivers. If you are searching for the ultimate Kasol and Manali tour package from Delhi, this 6-Day / 5-Night Offbeat Himalayan Escape by Awara Banjara is curated for modern nomads and backpackers craving authenticity over rushed tourist checklists. Leaving the routine chaos behind on an overnight semi-sleeper AC Volvo journey from Delhi, you step straight into the slow, soulful rhythm of the Himalayas. Discover the scenic forest trails leading to the roaring Jogini Waterfall, experience the peaceful cedar-draped courtyard of the historic Hadimba Devi Temple, and unwind through the vibrant, acoustic-filled cafe trails of Old Manali. The adventure ascends as we drive through the world-famous Atal Tunnel into the dramatic, high-altitude terrain of Lahaul Valley to witness the raw majesty of Sissu Waterfall and Solang Valley. Head down towards Kullu for white-water rafting and paragliding thrills before winding into the mystic pine corridors of Parvati Valley. Experience Kasol’s iconic riverside cafes, the tranquil pine-lined walk to Chalal village, and the therapeutic natural hot sulfur springs of Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara. Evenings are reserved for starlit skies, crackling bonfires, live music, and bonding with your new travel tribe. Designed with verified local stays, scenic transfers, curated meals, and experienced trip leaders, this is more than a holiday—it’s an unforgettable Himalayan journey. Pack your bags and experience the real mountains with Awara Banjara!

Itinerary

Gather at the designated pickup point in Delhi by evening and meet your fellow banjaras. Board the comfortable AC Volvo as we leave behind the chaotic city buzz and roll towards the misty pine forests of Himachal. Feel the night breeze turn crisp and mountain-fresh as conversations start flowing. Overnight journey on the winding Himalayan roads under starlit skies.

Wake up to the roaring Beas River and dramatic valley views as we pull into Manali. Check-in to our cozy stay, freshen up, and take in the slow mountain rhythm. Later, set out on a scenic walk through the apple orchards up to the roaring Jogini Waterfall, followed by a soulful visit to the age-old Hadimba Devi Temple wrapped in giant cedar trees. As dusk settles, stroll down Mall Road and spend the evening soaking in the bohemian energy of Old Manali cafes with warm chai and live acoustic tunes. Overnight stay in Manali.

Fuel up with a hearty breakfast and gear up for an unforgettable high-altitude drive. We cross the engineering marvel, Atal Tunnel, transitioning from lush green slopes into the raw, dramatic, snow-draped landscapes of Lahaul Valley. Explore the majestic Sissu Waterfall and soak in the raw nomadic silence of the mountains. On our way back, stop by Solang Valley for panoramic views and thrilling snow vibes before returning to Manali for a warm, community dinner. Overnight stay in Manali.

Bid farewell to Manali and hit the road towards Kullu. For the thrill-seekers, charge your adrenaline with white-water rafting in the icy Beas or soar high in the skies with paragliding (own expense). By afternoon, the valley shifts colors as we enter the mystic, pine-scented corridor of Kasol. Check-in to our riverside stay, sit by the gushing Parvati River, hop through aesthetic Israeli cafes, and gather around a cozy bonfire under the starry night sky with acoustic jams and stories. Overnight stay in Kasol/Parvati Valley.

Start the day with peaceful vibes by visiting the revered Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara and dip your feet in the naturally therapeutic hot sulfur springs. Later, take a serene walk along the iconic suspension bridge to Chalal village—breathing in the damp pine scent and exploring hidden graffiti cafes. Pick up dreamcatchers, boho woolens, and raw souvenirs from Kasol market. By evening, gather your backpacks with a heavy heart and board your return Volvo from Bhuntar/Kasol back to Delhi.

Reach Delhi early in the morning. Say goodbye to your mountain tribe with a camera roll full of aesthetic memories, tired legs, and a refreshed nomadic soul until the next adventure calls.
