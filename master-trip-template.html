About Awara Banjara

Awara Banjara is a premier adventure travel company headquartered in Manali, Himachal Pradesh. Specializing in high-altitude expeditions, bespoke road trips, and immersive cultural tours, we pride ourselves on delivering seamless and safe Himalayan experiences. Our itineraries are crafted with local expertise and a deep commitment to sustainable tourism. Our focus is on providing high-quality service, expert-led treks, and an invite-only community experience that ensures every journey is as exclusive as it is adventurous. With Awara Banjara, you aren't just visiting a destination; you are experiencing it with those who know it best.

Detailed Itinerary

  • Board a premium overnight Volvo coach departing from Delhi in the late evening.
  • Relax in executive reclining seats as the vehicle handles the overnight highway journey up into the Himalayas.
  • Morning arrival in Manali; check-in to your boutique mountain retreat for a curated breakfast.
  • Step into a comfortable, dedicated private cab/Traveller for a relaxed valley tour to let your body adjust smoothly to the altitude.
  • Enjoy an effortless drive past cedar forests to Hadimba Temple, take a scenic walk to Jogni Waterfall, and spend the evening exploring Mall Road.
  • Board your Ride in Manali, drive up through Solang Valley, and execute a dramatic transit through the world-famous Atal Tunnel.
  • Drive past massive rock walls, hanging glaciers, and sweeping curves along the newly opened Lahaul-Zanskar corridor.
  • Arrive at the base of the sacred, towering monolith Gonbo Rangjon.
  • Relax through the night at an exclusive private camping setup for an intimate private bonfire and celestial stargazing under the Milky Way.
  • Conquer the rugged, raw riverside dirt tracks from the monolith to the isolated village of Purne.
  • Embark on a curated, private riverside trek along the Tsarap River.
  • Explore the magnificent 12th-century Phuktal Gompa, beautifully built directly into a cliffside cave sanctuary.
  • Trek back to your dropping point and ride a sweeping, high-altitude loop to your lodge in Jispa.
  • Enjoy a late-morning start, riding smoothly through Keylong alongside the calming Bhaga River.
  • Cross back over the mountain passes with flexible, personalized stops for photographs and local sea-buckthorn tea.
  • Arrive in Manali by mid-afternoon to conclude the circuit and unwind at a lounge.
  • Board your premium overnight Volvo coach in the evening for a comfortable, resting return transit back down to the capital.
  • Early morning arrival at the designated drop point in Delhi.
  • The high-octane mountain expedition officially concludes with seamless logistics completed and lifelong memories secured.
