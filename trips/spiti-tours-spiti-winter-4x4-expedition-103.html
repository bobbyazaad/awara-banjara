Itinerary

Leaving the Plains Behind — Board your overnight transit vehicle (Volvo/Tempo Traveller) from Delhi, leaving the bustling urban plains behind. Sit back, relax, and transition into the rhythm of the mountains as the overnight drive winds upward into the scenic Himalayan foothills, setting the stage for the raw, snow-draped winter wilderness awaiting you in Spiti with Awara Banjara.

Entering Kinnaur's Frozen Lap — Arrive in Shimla early in the morning to meet your expedition captain and crew. Transition into comfortable 4x4 vehicles specially geared to handle challenging winter terrain and black-ice conditions. Embark on a dramatic drive through the Kinnaur region, tracing the roaring Sutlej River before climbing into the breathtaking Sangla Valley. Explore local landscapes and snow-blanketed wooden houses, with your overnight stay arranged in Sangla or Chitkul near the border.

Into the Cold Desert — Depart Chitkul and begin an awe-inspiring exploration drive deeper into the Trans-Himalayan rain shadow region toward Tabo. En route, stop at the serene Nako village to witness its high-altitude lake frozen solid into a shimmering sheet of ice. Next, take a fascinating detour to Gue village to view the remarkable 500-year-old naturally mummified monk preserved at the local monastery. Continue your 4x4 expedition across stark, moon-like winter landscapes before arriving in Tabo for a warm, starlit overnight stay.

The Ajanta of the Himalayas — Begin your morning exploring the ancient Tabo Monastery, revered as the "Ajanta of the Himalayas" for its priceless 1,000-year-old frescoes and mud-brick heritage. After immersing yourself in its tranquil, spiritual aura, hit the rugged tracks toward Kaza. Along the way, take a thrilling 4x4 detour to the cliffside Dhankar Monastery, dramatically perched on a jagged ridge overlooking the frozen confluence of the Spiti and Pin rivers. Arrive in Kaza, the high-altitude headquarters of Spiti, for your overnight stay.

Hikkim, Komic & Langza — Dedicate the day to conquering Spiti’s highest motorable loops and remote nomadic settlements surrounding Kaza. Navigate snow-lined tracks to dispatch a postcard from Hikkim, home to the world’s highest operational post office. Continue driving up to Komic, one of the highest permanently inhabited villages on earth, and admire the majestic giant Buddha statue keeping watch over the fossil-rich valley of Langza. Return to Kaza by evening to share stories by the heater and enjoy your overnight stay.

Hanging Over the Abyss — After breakfast, put your 4x4 capability to the test on high-altitude mountain passes as you drive toward the iconic, snow-draped Key Monastery perched dramatically on a hilltop fortress. Explore Kibber wildlife territory, then cross the engineering marvel of the Chicham Bridge, suspended precariously over a deep, dizzying gorge. Spend the afternoon soaking in the dramatic winter panoramas of the upper Spiti valley before returning to Kaza for your final night in the heartland.

Retracing the Kinnaur Trail — Begin your return journey, heading back down through the rugged terrain toward the lush pine forests and charming village of Kalpa. The scenic winter drive offers shifting perspectives of towering rock faces and distant snowfields. Upon arrival in Kalpa, witness the magical phenomenon of the sacred Kinner Kailash mountain range glowing fiery gold and pink as the sun sets. Spend a peaceful evening wrapped in the quiet beauty of Kinnaur and settle in for an overnight stay.

Descent Through the Gorge — Depart Kalpa and navigate the winding mountain roads back down through the Kinnaur valley toward Shimla. Enjoy your final shifting glimpses of high Himalayan crags as the altitude drops and green valleys return. Upon reaching Shimla in the evening, board your overnight return transit vehicle back toward Delhi, giving you time to unwind and reflect on an unforgettable winter expedition.

Carrying the Banjara Spirit Home — Arrive early in the morning back in Delhi, marking the successful conclusion of your 8-night / 9-day Spiti winter 4x4 expedition. Head back to your onward destination carrying unforgettable memories, raw photographs of frozen Himalayan passes, and the timeless, untamed spirit of the mountains.
