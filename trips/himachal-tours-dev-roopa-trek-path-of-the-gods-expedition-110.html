About This Trip

Awara Banjara brings you the sacred "Path of the Gods: Dev Roopa Expedition"—an 8-day high-altitude trekking experience in the heart of Himachal Pradesh. Starting from Delhi to Manali, trek through Malana Dam, Behali, and Mota Grahan before reaching the pristine, sacred alpine meadow of Dev Roopa. Enjoy expert guidance... Read More

Itinerary

Board the AC Volvo bus from Delhi for an overnight journey to Manali.

Arrive in Manali, check-in to your stay, register for the trek, and go on a short acclimatization walk.

Drive from Manali to Malana Dam and begin the trek towards Behali campsite.

Trek through scenic trails and pine forests to reach the riverbed campsite at Mota Grahan.

Trek to the sacred alpine plateau of Dev Roopa. Explore the expansive grasslands and descend back to Mota Grahan.

Descend back through the valley from Mota Grahan to Behali campsite.

Final trek down to the roadhead, drive to Bhuntar, and board the overnight Volvo back to Delhi.

Reach Delhi in the morning with unforgettable memories of the Dev Roopa Expedition.
