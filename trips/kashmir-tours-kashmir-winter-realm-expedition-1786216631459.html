Itinerary

Land in Srinagar and step into your floating wooden heritage houseboat on Dal Lake. Spend your afternoon wandering through the timeless Mughal Gardens—Shalimar and Nishat—then soak in the raw, golden hour serenity of the mountains during a 1-hour sunset Shikara ride across the mirrored waters.

Journey through frozen willow-lined roads into the high-altitude bowl of Gulmarg. Ride the world-renowned Phase 1 Gondola cable car up into the snowfields, explore the sweeping alpine meadows, and feel the nomadic rush of high-altitude winter sports and powder snow.

Head out via local 4x4 trails to witness the dramatic, ice-sculpted frozen cascades of Drung Waterfall. Drive through the historic saffron fields of Pampore and along the Jhelum riverbed to reach the scenic mountain town of Pahalgam.

Dive deep into the pristine wilderness of Pahalgam. Explore the lush cinematic stretches of Aru Valley, the dramatic gorges of Betaab Valley, and the glacial threshold of Chandanwari before an evening return drive to Srinagar.

Escape the ordinary with a soulful trek through silent pine forests from Nilnag Lake up to the sprawling alpine meadows of Yusmarg (or navigate the pristine snowscapes of Doodhpathri during heavy snowfall), returning to Srinagar by nightfall.

Enjoy a hot, locally spiced morning kahwa on the deck, bid adieu to your fellow Banjara travelers, and check out for your transfer to Srinagar Airport, carrying memories that will call you back to the mountains.
