About This Trip

The Untamed Frontier: Where the Road Ends, the Soul Awakes There is the Himalayas you see on postcards, and then there is Zanskar—a mystical, razor-sharp trans-Himalayan realm sculpted by roaring glacial rivers, towering vertical monoliths, and ancient Buddhist serenity. Hidden behind formidable high-altitude mountain passes, Zanskar is not just a destination; it is an initiation into the raw, unhurried wild. Our Escape to Zanskar (6-Day Expedition) is curated for the true wanderers—those who refuse to be bound by generic tourist itineraries and seek journeys that leave dust on their boots and fire in their spirits. Spanning across legendary mountain corridors—from the verdant cedar valleys of Manali to the windswept heights of Shinku La Pass (16,580 ft), the sacred shadow of Gonbo Rangjon, and the mythical cliffside cave hermitage of Phuktal Gompa—this is trans-Himalayan road tripping in its purest, most authentic form. The Awara Banjara Way: How We Travel At Awara Banjara, we do not sell standardized package holidays. We curate community-led, raw mountain expeditions. Nomadic Energy & Authentic Tribe Vibe: You don’t travel with a coach of strangers; you journey with a tribe of like-minded explorers. From sharing stories around crackling riverside bonfires in Jispa to gazing at the silver canopy of the Milky Way beside the holy monolith of Gonbo Rangjon, our journeys forge lifelong bonds. Safety First, Always Grounded: Raw adventure does not mean reckless travel. We build our expeditions with dedicated acclimatization hikes (like the Jogini Waterfall trail in Manali), seasoned trans-Himalayan expedition captains, certified 4x4/tempo logistics, and on-board emergency oxygen and high-altitude medical support. Slow, Immersive Storytelling: We believe in pausing where the mass crowds rush. We let you soak in the acoustic reverberations of 12th-century monastic chants inside the caves of Phuktal Gompa, taste authentic local butter tea with mountain elders, and wake up to unfiltered golden sunrises over glacial monoliths. Key Expedition Highlights Conquer Shinku La Pass (16,580 ft): Ascend the formidable high-mountain pass connecting Lahaul Valley to the secluded gorges of Zanskar, cutting through rugged terrains and snow-carved ridges. Camp in the Shadow of Gonbo Rangjon: Experience an unforgettable, high-altitude starlit night camp beneath the sacred standalone monolith revered by locals as the protective deity of the valley. Trek to the Cliff-Hanging Phuktal Cave Monastery: Embark on a dramatic hike along the turquoise Tsarap River to reach one of the world's most remote Buddhist cave monasteries, carved directly into a sheer limestone cliff. Riverside Solace in Jispa & Lahaul: Unwind along the turquoise waters of the Bhaga River, breathing in pristine alpine air away from all urban noise. Ready to Answer the Mountain's Call? Zanskar is wild, unforgiving, and breathtakingly beautiful. If you are seeking an expedition that strips away the ordinary, challenges your boundaries, and reconnects your soul with the raw rhythm of the earth, your seat in the tribe is waiting. Pack your rucksack, leave the city behind, and Escape to Zanskar the Awara Banjara way.

Itinerary

The Raw Escape Begins. Your high-altitude Himalayan quest begins in Delhi as you board the comfortable overnight AC Volvo semi-sleeper coach (from Majnu ka Tilla / ISBT Kashmiri Gate). Leave behind the daily city noise and traffic as the wheels roll into the northern plains and start climbing the pine-scented foothills of Himachal Pradesh. Settle in, unwind, and prepare your soul for the untamed roads of Zanskar.

Wake up to the crisp mountain breeze in Manali. After checking into our mountain stay and freshening up, head out for a gentle acclimatization hike through dense pine forests to the roaring Jogini Waterfalls. Soak in the misty views, pay respects at the historic Hadimba Devi Temple, and spend a lazy evening wandering Old Manali's cafes and Mall Road. Later in the evening, join your Trip Captain for the mandatory Expedition Briefing covering AMS altitude precautions, Zanskar terrain safety protocols, and route roadmap over a hot, authentic dinner. Overnight stay in Manali.

The real trans-Himalayan overland adventure kicks off at dawn. Drive through the marvel of Atal Tunnel and enter the rugged barren beauty of Lahaul Valley. Pass through Sissu and Darcha before testing your limits at the formidable Shinku La Pass (16,580 ft)—the gateway to Ladakh’s Zanskar region. Descend into the untouched Lungnak Valley to reach the colossal, stand-alone holy monolith of Gonbo Rangjon. Settle into our riverside eco-campsite and experience a night of jaw-dropping raw wilderness under the glowing Milky Way and a million stars.

Wake up to the early morning sun casting golden reflections on the holy Gonbo Rangjon monolith. Post breakfast, take an exhilarating off-road transit to the trail-head and begin the iconic trek along the turquoise Tsarap/Lungnak river to Phuktal Gompa (Phugtal Monastery)—a legendary 12th-century Buddhist cave hermitage hanging dramatically from a vertical limestone cliff. Absorb the profound cave chants, trek back, and drive back across Shinku La to reach the serene riverside stays of Jispa. Unwind with a cozy campsite bonfire and hearty mountain meal. Overnight stay in Jispa.

Savor a slow morning with hot tea by the gushing Bhaga river in Jispa. Start our scenic return drive along the rugged Lahaul tracks, capturing final postcard-worthy mountain frames before cruising through the Atal Tunnel back into Manali. Enjoy free afternoon time in Manali for local shopping, authentic Himachali souvenirs, and dinner. In the late evening, board your overnight semi-sleeper Volvo coach back to Delhi with stories of a lifetime.

Morning Drop-off & Memories for Life. Arrive in Delhi in the morning at the drop-off hub. Return home with dust on your boots, deep mountain bonds, epic wide-angle landscapes, and the untamed spirit of the Awara Banjara tribe.
